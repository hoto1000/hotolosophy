# Organizations to consider

Not endorsements. Check each on an evaluator such as Charity Navigator before donating.

- Trafficking: Polaris Project, International Justice Mission, Anti-Slavery International
- Child exploitation: Thorn, NCMEC, WeProtect Global Alliance
- War and conflict relief: ICRC, Doctors Without Borders (MSF), UNHCR
- Peace research: International Crisis Group, Uppsala Conflict Data Program
