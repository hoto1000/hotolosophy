# Hotlines and reporting channels

Verified against official sites on 2026-10-08. Hours and numbers can change; check the source.

| Need | Channel | Source |
|---|---|---|
| US human trafficking | 1-888-373-7888 (TTY 711), text 233733, live chat, 24/7 | humantraffickinghotline.org |
| UK modern slavery | 08000 121 700 (currently 8am to 6pm daily). Emergency: 999 | modernslaveryhelpline.org |
| Child exploitation (US) | NCMEC CyberTipline, report.cybertip.org, 1-800-843-5678 (1-800-THE-LOST), 24h | report.cybertip.org |
| Child sexual abuse imagery online (UK/international) | Internet Watch Foundation, anonymous, iwf.org.uk/en/uk-report/ | iwf.org.uk |
| Crisis support (US) | Call or text 988 | 988lifeline.org |

Other countries: search "[country] human trafficking hotline" on an official government or well-known NGO site.

If you see child sexual abuse material: do not download, share, or investigate. Report the URL and stop.
